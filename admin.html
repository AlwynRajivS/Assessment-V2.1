🤖 AI Question Generator

Generate MCQs from a topic or study material using Groq or Google Gemini. Pick how many questions, which Bloom's Taxonomy levels, and optionally which skill-enhancement question styles you want, then review and choose which ones to add to your test.

Supports .txt, .md, .csv, .pdf, .docx (Word) and .pptx (PowerPoint) — text is extracted right here in your browser, nothing is uploaded anywhere. Legacy .doc/.ppt aren't supported; re-save as .docx/.pptx first, or paste the text directly.
These are optional question-style flavors (Scenario-based, Case Study, Critical Thinking, etc.) layered on top of the Bloom's levels above. 📋 Quick Start Guide

Setup Steps:
1. Create a test with title and duration
2. Add questions manually, or use "AI Generate" to create MCQs from a topic/material with Groq or Gemini AI
3. Set test as ACTIVE and configure dates
4. Get link & QR code to share with students
5. Monitor leaderboard in real-time
6. Generate reports after exam window closes
